Your day, in one frame.

DayFrame is a desktop-first app for Windows and Mac that brings your email, calendar, contacts, and more together in one place — with a friendly layout and a Windows Phone-inspired live tile navigation rail for switching between modules.

One shell. Every part of your day.

Instead of juggling separate apps, DayFrame gives every part of your personal world a home behind a single shell. A persistent tile rail on the left keeps your modules one tap away — bold, square, unmistakably blue — while the main pane shows whatever you're focused on right now.

We want you to feel at home in DayFrame, so even if you don't like the colors or fonts in the application, they're all customizable. Tiles can be any color or size that you'd like.

Under the hood, each module is an independently built, independently tested piece that plugs into a shared contract layer. That's what lets DayFrame grow — new modules arrive over time without disturbing the ones you already rely on.
