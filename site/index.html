Your day at a glance. Your world in tiles.

DayFrame is a desktop-first app for Windows that brings your email, calendar, contacts, and more together in one place — with a friendly layout and a Windows Phone-inspired live tile navigation rail for switching between modules. Mac support is planned.

What's new in 1.0 Preview 2

One shell. Every part of your day.

Instead of juggling separate apps, DayFrame gives every part of your personal world a home behind a single shell. A persistent tile rail on the left keeps your modules one tap away — bold, square, and yours to color — while the main pane shows whatever you're focused on right now.

Pinning goes deeper than modules, too. A single RSS feed, a board, a world clock — pin any of them straight to the rail and it becomes its own live tile.

We want you to feel at home in DayFrame, so even if you don't like the colors or fonts in the application, they're all customizable. Tiles can be any color or size that you'd like.

Under the hood, each module is an independently built, independently tested piece that plugs into a shared contract layer. That's what lets DayFrame grow — new modules arrive over time without disturbing the ones you already rely on.

1.0 Preview 2 · October 2026

What's new in 1.0 Preview 2

DayFrame 1.0 is a public preview, and anyone on Windows can install it from the Microsoft Store. Preview 2 is the first build to arrive through the Store's own updates. It brings a welcome tour, a Twitch module, boards you lay out freely, a much richer Weather, tiles that sparkle, and a long list of speed and polish work.

In the Microsoft Store

Install DayFrame from the Microsoft Store and the Store keeps it up to date. The package is signed, so there's no SmartScreen warning to click through. It runs on Windows 10 and 11, on both x64 and Arm64 PCs.

Already running the zip build from a private preview? The first time the Store version starts, it brings your settings and accounts across.

A welcome on first start

A new welcome tour introduces every module and lets you pick which ones to turn on. It also covers appearance and getting around, and you can reopen it any time from the Application menu. Fewer modules start switched on, and Email, Calendar and Contacts say plainly how to connect an account rather than sitting empty.

Twitch, for streamers

Sign in with a short device code, and your channel's tile shows when you're live and how many people are watching. The Twitch dashboard adds your stream title, category, follower and subscriber totals and your schedule, plus which of the channels you follow are live right now.

Boards on a free grid

Boards now lay widgets out on a grid you control: drag a widget anywhere and resize it from any side. Repositories you watch on GitHub can go on a board as widgets too. If one widget can't load, only that card says so, and the rest of the board carries on.

Weather in detail

The Weather module adds today's details, the next 24 hours and a seven-day chance of rain, and you can switch between your locations. The tile's back face splits into the days ahead, and the tile icon can show the condition and the temperature together.

Tiles that sparkle (and drip)

Five glitter textures (silver, gold, red, green and blue) twinkle on the rail, and the new Green Slime material drips and wobbles. The light theme gets a light navigation pane, toolbar and title bar to match, and the tile menu is grouped by what you're trying to do.

At home in Windows

  • Opening DayFrame again brings up the window you already have, and the taskbar jump list opens Email, Calendar, Contacts or Notes directly.
  • Your tiles' counts add up to a badge on the taskbar icon.
  • DayFrame can start when you sign in to Windows. It's off unless you turn it on.
  • The title bar stays readable in the dark theme.

Faster, lighter, and better behaved

  • Google Calendar and Contacts and CalDAV/CardDAV servers sync only what changed, and cached mail, events and posts stay available offline.
  • Notes keeps its index between runs, and long contact lists scroll smoothly with an A-Z jump.
  • GitHub pull requests show their check status. In Email you can hide preview text and archive from the keyboard. RSS can follow a subreddit.
  • The Help page answers questions you type about DayFrame.

Built to grow, module by module

Seven everyday modules start on, seven more are a switch away in Settings, and Boards pulls them all together. More arrive over time.

Email, Calendar & Contacts

These three share one connection per account — a Google sign-in, or IMAP, CalDAV and CardDAV for providers such as Yahoo, Posteo, Mailbox.org and Zoho, or your own server.

GitHub

Pick repositories from a list instead of typing owner/name, and set how often each one polls. An issue and PR feed keeps comments in sync and supports filters, alongside a pane of monitored repositories and saved views — all within GitHub's rate limits.

RSS

A three-pane reader where feeds show their own channel logo. Pin any feed to the rail as its own tile, carrying that logo with it. Remote content is sandboxed, and images stay blocked until you allow them.

Time

World-clock tiles for the places you care about. Pin any clock to the rail, and copy a time in several formats when you need to share it.

Weather, Market News & Bluesky

Each has its own module view for a closer look, plus a matching board widget for your dashboards.

Boards: your own dashboards

Boards are customizable dashboards built from widgets drawn out of your modules — arrange the pieces of your day exactly how you want to see them.

Widgets available today: Calendar day view, Calendar agenda, Weather conditions, Bluesky recent posts, Market News watchlist, RSS, and Email folder.

  • A distinct edit mode for laying widgets out
  • Undo/redo, plus a whole-board reset
  • Renameable widgets
  • Per-board refresh cadence and density
  • Full keyboard support
  • Pin any board to the tile rail as its own tile

Make it feel like yours

Feeling at home in DayFrame is a first-class goal, not an afterthought. Personalization goes well beyond swapping a color.

Eight board style presets ship today, ready to apply in one click: Midnight, Paper, Neon Tokyo, Atari 2600, Dragon, Fairy, Mermaid, and Hot Dog Stand.

  • Your own wallpaper on a board
  • Texture backgrounds for boards and widget headers
  • Board-wide and per-widget typography
  • Widget header colors with automatically derived, readable foregrounds
  • Built-in contrast checking
  • Customizable tile chrome
  • A library of tile textures, and any tile color you like

Accessibility — contrast and legibility — is preserved across every theme.

Get DayFrame

DayFrame 1.0 Preview is free in the Microsoft Store for Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) and Windows 11, on x64 and Arm64 PCs. The Store installs it and keeps it up to date.

This is a public preview. The people who build DayFrame use it every day, and it still has rough edges. Google sign-in shows an "unverified app" notice while Google's review is in progress, and is limited to the first 100 users. Gmail also works as an IMAP account with an app password.
