Your day at a glance. Your world in tiles.

DayFrame is a desktop-first app for Windows that brings your email, calendar, contacts, and more together in one place — with a friendly layout and a Windows Phone-inspired live tile navigation rail for switching between modules. Mac support is planned.

See what's new in v0.6 Join the preview

One shell. Every part of your day.

Instead of juggling separate apps, DayFrame gives every part of your personal world a home behind a single shell. A persistent tile rail on the left keeps your modules one tap away — bold, square, and yours to color — while the main pane shows whatever you're focused on right now.

Pinning goes deeper than modules, too. A single RSS feed, a board, a world clock — pin any of them straight to the rail and it becomes its own live tile.

We want you to feel at home in DayFrame, so even if you don't like the colors or fonts in the application, they're all customizable. Tiles can be any color or size that you'd like.

Under the hood, each module is an independently built, independently tested piece that plugs into a shared contract layer. That's what lets DayFrame grow — new modules arrive over time without disturbing the ones you already rely on.

What's new in v0.6

v0.6 is the biggest DayFrame release so far, and the last private preview before the first public one. Three months of work landed in it: email that works with any mail provider, calendar and contacts on open standards, four new modules, and a long list of stability fixes aimed at the day strangers install the app for the first time.

Email for any provider, and more than one of them

Email is no longer Gmail-only. Add any IMAP account, with ready-made presets for Yahoo, AOL, Posteo, Mailbox.org, Zoho, WEB.DE, GMX, Telekom, freenet and Vivaldi — or type a bare host name and let Find settings discover the rest. Connect several accounts side by side, each with its own tile and its own From when you compose.

Compose with rich formatting, pin important messages to the top of a folder, open two messages side by side in split view, mark a whole folder read, and turn a message into a contact or a calendar event without leaving it.

Calendar and Contacts on open standards

Calendar speaks CalDAV and Contacts speaks CardDAV, so your Posteo, Mailbox.org, Zoho or self-hosted server works alongside Google. Repeating events can be edited and deleted, a new event goes into the calendar you choose, and sync is incremental so a refresh costs seconds, not minutes.

One connection is shared across Email, Calendar and Contacts. Sign in once, and DayFrame asks before removing an account another module still depends on.

Four new modules

  • Asana — My Tasks grouped by due date, project activity, task detail, and write actions: complete, reschedule, comment, quick-add. Pin a project or a single task to the rail, and drop Focus, Workload, Week-ahead and Due-today widgets on a board.
  • Mastodon — sign in to any instance for your home timeline and notifications, favorite and bookmark posts, and pin a saved hashtag to the rail.
  • Sports — live scores, finals and headlines for the NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, college football and the major international football competitions. Favorite a team and it leads the tile.
  • Time — a glanceable clock tile for the zones you care about, with a right-click to copy the current time in the format you need.

Boards that know who you mean

A board widget can now be bound to a person, and it renders everything every module knows about them — their mail, their events, their tasks — on one card. Bluesky saved searches, Mastodon hashtags, Sports teams and Asana projects all arrive as new widgets, and your pinned tiles live in Appearance settings beside the tile overrides.

Notes, hardened

The Markdown notes module that debuted in v0.5 gained inline [[ wikilink autocomplete while you type, and a long list of fixes from a line-by-line review: renames that never leave a dangling link, line endings that survive an edit, and an editor that never throws away unsaved work silently. Your vault stays yours — it is plain files on disk, and Obsidian can open it at the same time.

Tiles with more personality

A module's logo can fill its tile or sit centered with an adjustable title strip. Pick any rail color from a Paint-style picker, collapse empty rows from the rail's context menu, and the live tiles now pre-scale their textures and pause their flip clock when the window is hidden, so a full rail costs less than it did.

Built for the first public install

  • Your local cache is encrypted at rest, and nothing waits on it before the first frame.
  • A rolling diagnostics log, a log folder and Copy diagnostics in About, plus crash handling that tells you when the app didn't shut down cleanly.
  • Sync starts on launch with the visible tiles first, and modules back off politely when a provider rate-limits them.
  • GitHub signs in with the device flow, so the app ships no client secret, and a Google account can be disconnected — and revoked — from inside DayFrame.
  • Windows builds for both x64 and Arm64.

Email, Calendar & Contacts

These three share one connection per account — a Google sign-in, or IMAP, CalDAV and CardDAV for providers such as Yahoo, Posteo, Mailbox.org and Zoho, or your own server.

GitHub

Pick repositories from a list instead of typing owner/name, and set how often each one polls. An issue and PR feed keeps comments in sync and supports filters, alongside a pane of monitored repositories and saved views — all within GitHub's rate limits.

RSS

A three-pane reader where feeds show their own channel logo. Pin any feed to the rail as its own tile, carrying that logo with it. Remote content is sandboxed, and images stay blocked until you allow them.

Time

World-clock tiles for the places you care about. Pin any clock to the rail, and copy a time in several formats when you need to share it.

Weather, Market News & Bluesky

Each has its own module view for a closer look, plus a matching board widget for your dashboards.

Boards: your own dashboards

Boards are customizable dashboards built from widgets drawn out of your modules — arrange the pieces of your day exactly how you want to see them.

Widgets available today: Calendar day view, Calendar agenda, Weather conditions, Bluesky recent posts, Market News watchlist, RSS, and Email folder.

  • A distinct edit mode for laying widgets out
  • Undo/redo, plus a whole-board reset
  • Renameable widgets
  • Per-board refresh cadence and density
  • Full keyboard support
  • Pin any board to the tile rail as its own tile

Make it feel like yours

Feeling at home in DayFrame is a first-class goal, not an afterthought. Personalization goes well beyond swapping a color.

Eight board style presets ship today, ready to apply in one click: Midnight, Paper, Neon Tokyo, Atari 2600, Dragon, Fairy, Mermaid, and Hot Dog Stand.

  • Your own wallpaper on a board
  • Texture backgrounds for boards and widget headers
  • Board-wide and per-widget typography
  • Widget header colors with automatically derived, readable foregrounds
  • Built-in contrast checking
  • Customizable tile chrome
  • A library of tile textures, and any tile color you like

Accessibility — contrast and legibility — is preserved across every theme.
