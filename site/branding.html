Brand Assets

Writing about DayFrame, featuring it in a video, or linking to it from your site? Use these files. Each one is free to use when you're talking about DayFrame.

Wordmark

The wordmark is the mark and the name together. It's the first choice wherever there's room for it. Pick the version that matches the background it sits on.

Logo mark

The mark on its own is a frame of tiles with today lit up in the middle. Use it where the wordmark won't fit: avatars, app grids, favicons and small placements.

Using the logo

  • Write the name as DayFrame: one word, capital D, capital F.
  • Leave clear space around the logo at least as wide as one of the mark's small tiles.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, recolor, outline or add effects to the mark or wordmark.
  • Don't rebuild the wordmark in another typeface. Use the files on this page.
  • Use the light version on light backgrounds and the dark version on dark ones, so "Frame" always stays readable.
  • Don't use the logo in a way that suggests DayFrame sponsors or endorses you or your product.
